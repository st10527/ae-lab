## Core Focus & Technical Highlights (核心研究與技術重點)

1. **Multimodal Edge Sensing & Inference (高階邊緣多模態影像推論與感知推理)**
   * Deploys high-performance edge multimodal inference accelerators and industrial-grade micro edge sensing gateway nodes directly at construction sites.
   * Performs real-time hazard perception and safety reasoning at the network edge without relying on cloud servers.
2. **Resilient Disconnected Communication (斷網通訊機制整合)**
   * Addresses harsh construction environments where external communication links are unstable or unavailable.
   * Integrates resilient local/delay-tolerant networking protocols with edge AIoT gateway nodes to guarantee reliable delivery of critical safety alerts even under network disconnection.
3. **Cross-Disciplinary Field Validation (資工 × 土木跨領域場域驗證)**
   * Combines computer science expertise in Edge AIoT and wireless networking with civil engineering domain knowledge for real-world construction site deployment and validation.
