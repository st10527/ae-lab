abstract: "Autonomous Aerial Vehicles (AAVs) operating in Delay-Tolerant Networks (DTNs) face significant challenges from three-dimensional environmental wind fields and limited onboard battery capacity. This paper presents a wind-aware 3D AAV-DTN communication framework integrated with dynamic cooperative Wireless Power Transfer (WPT). By extending opportunistic network simulation into a 3D aerial environment with realistic wind field dynamics, we model the impact of wind on flight trajectories, stability, and propulsion energy consumption. Furthermore, cooperative WPT mechanisms are introduced among aerial nodes to share energy dynamically during missions, significantly reducing transmission delay and improving overall network lifetime under varying wind conditions."
