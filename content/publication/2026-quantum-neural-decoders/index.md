abstract: "Neural decoders for quantum error correction are trained on logical labels that are intrinsically noisy: owing to degeneracy, the logical outcome remains random given the syndrome. We replace these sampled labels with posterior soft targets. For the distance-3 surface code under circuit-level noise, an exact posterior shows that the label variance reaches 80% of the optimal logical error rate. Posterior targets reduce the excess logical error of neural decoders and, combined with training at an inflated error rate, lower it five- to sixfold relative to the best sampled-label training. Posteriors computed from a misestimated noise model work equally well."
